Creating the Trust between on-premises management server and the Check Point Portal.

a. Return to SmartConsole and click on the Get Token button.

b. Switch back to Firefox and log in, choose the account you just created, and click Next.

c. Select the Checkbox to link your Self-hosted SMS to the portal and click Next.

d. Click COPY TOKEN and return to SmartConsole, then paste in the token.

e. Move the Connect your Security Gateways to Check Point Portal slider to ON, ensure that below in the Gateways Connection Details section, the All option is selected, and that in the Establish connection to Security Gateways, the Immediately option is selected.

Click Connect.

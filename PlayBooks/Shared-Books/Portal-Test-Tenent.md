*Register new tenant*

1) Open a browser and go to https://portal.checkpoint.com/create-account

fill in the details. Even if you already have a portal login, you can create a new tenant for this exercise. Use a descriptive name such as “Workforce-AI-test-2026-myname” or similar.

a. If you do have a Portal login already:

i. Ensure that your corporate policies allow you to log into it from a non-corporate device. If you can login from a non-corporate device, go to Step 2 “Creating a new tenant account with an existing Portal login”

ii. If you are prevented from accessing your portal account, go to Step 3 “Creating a new Portal login” and create a new account with a non-corporate email address that you have access to (follow the process below to do this).

b. If you do not have a Portal account, go to Step 3 “Creating a new Portal login”.
